# Flat Repair

Most visits are flat tires. A careful flat repair takes about twenty
minutes once the wheel is off the bike.

## Steps

1. Remove the wheel and lever off one side of the tire bead.
2. Pull the tube and find the hole by listening or using the water tub.
3. Check the inside of the tire for glass or wire before fitting a tube.
4. Seat the tire, inflate slowly, and check the bead line all around.
