# Intake and Check-In

Intake is where every repair starts. The greeter records the rider, the
bike, and the problem before any work begins.

## Intake Steps

1. Greet the rider and ask what the bike needs.
2. Fill in an intake tag with the rider name, bike color, and the date.
3. Walk the rider to an open workstand, as described in [Doc 3](doc-03.md).
4. Collect the stand fee before work starts, using the rates in
   [Doc 10](doc-10.md).

## Tags and Tickets

Each bike gets a numbered tag. The tag stays with the bike until the
rider leaves. If the bike needs a full service rather than a single
repair, book it as a tune-up under [Doc 5](doc-05.md). The background of
the workshop and its opening hours are in [Doc 1](doc-01.md).
