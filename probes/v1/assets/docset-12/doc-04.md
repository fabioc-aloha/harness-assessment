# Safety Rules

Safety comes before speed. Any helper can stop a job that looks unsafe,
and the shift lead backs that call every time.

## Safety

Wear closed shoes in the work area. Tie back long hair. Keep fingers
clear of the chain when the wheel is spinning. Use the stand clamp as
described in [Doc 3](doc-03.md), and put the bike down before walking
away from the bench.

## Protective Gear

Gloves, eye protection, and aprons hang by the sink. Wash hands before
eating. The tool care routine that keeps guards and handles in good
shape is in [Doc 3, Tool Care](doc-03.md#tool-care).

## Incident Reports

Write up any injury or near miss on the incident form the same day. The
shift lead files the form with the closing records described in
[Doc 12](doc-12.md).
