# Welcome and Overview

The Maple Street Community Bike Workshop is a volunteer-run shop where
neighbors repair their own bikes with guidance from experienced helpers.
This handbook collects the procedures we use every week.

## Overview

New visitors start at the intake desk. The intake steps are described in
[Doc 2](doc-02.md). Everyone who picks up a tool follows the safety rules
in [Doc 4](doc-04.md). People who want to help on a regular basis should
read the volunteer guide in [Doc 9](doc-09.md).

## How the Workshop Runs

The shop opens three evenings a week and on Saturday mornings. Each
session has one shift lead, two mechanics, and one greeter. The shift
lead closes the shop using the checklist in [Doc 12](doc-12.md).
