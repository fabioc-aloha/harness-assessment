# Brakes and Gears

Brakes come first. A bike that cannot stop does not leave the shop, even
if the rider came in for something else.

## Brakes

Squeeze each lever. It should stop well before the bar. Replace pads
that are worn to the line, and keep hands clear of the rotor or rim
while the wheel spins, as [Doc 4, Safety](doc-04.md#safety) explains.
For cuts and scrapes, follow the steps in [Doc 4, First Aid](doc-04.md#first-aid) before the rider leaves.

## Gears

Shift through the full range under light pedaling. Adjust cable tension
at the barrel before reaching for limit screws. Stand setup and clamp
position are covered in [Doc 3](doc-03.md).
