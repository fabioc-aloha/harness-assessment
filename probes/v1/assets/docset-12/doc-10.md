# Fees and Donations

Stand time costs five dollars per hour. Members pay half. Nobody is
turned away for inability to pay; the greeter notes the waiver on the
intake tag, following the steps in [Doc 2](doc-02.md).

## Donations

Cash donations go in the locked box by the register. Used parts in good
shape go to the donation bin and are sorted during the monthly count
described in [Doc 8](doc-08.md).

## Spending

Parts orders are the main expense. The treasurer approves any single
order over one hundred dollars before it is placed.
