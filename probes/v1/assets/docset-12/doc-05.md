# Tune-Ups

A tune-up is a fixed sequence of checks and adjustments. Allow one hour
per bike and book tune-ups at intake, as described in [Doc 2](doc-02.md).

## Tune-Up Sequence

1. Clean the chain and check it for wear.
2. Adjust the brakes and gears, following [Doc 7](doc-07.md).
3. True the wheels and check spoke tension.
4. Pump the tires and check for cuts or dry rot.

## When a Tune-Up Finds More

If a tire will not hold air, switch to the flat repair steps in
[Doc 6](doc-06.md). If a part is worn out, check stock levels in
[Doc 8](doc-08.md) before promising a same-day finish.
