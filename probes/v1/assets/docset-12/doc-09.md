# Volunteers and Scheduling

Volunteers keep the shop running. New volunteers shadow an experienced
helper for two sessions before working a bench on their own.

## Signing Up

Add your name to the monthly roster at the front desk. Each session
needs one shift lead, two mechanics, and one greeter, as described in
[Doc 1](doc-01.md).

## Shift Swaps

Post swaps on the board by the door at least two days ahead. The shift
lead confirms every swap. Classes and open-shop nights that need extra
hands are listed in [Doc 11](doc-11.md).
